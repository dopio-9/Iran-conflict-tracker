THE ROUTE WAR, IN ONE SHOT

The Revolutionary Guard called it a “warning shot at a vessel on an unapproved route.” CENTCOM called it a “blatant attack” on a ship in international transit. Both are describing the same hole in the same engine room — and that dispute, not the fire itself, is the entire war now.

Iran did not close Bab-el-Mandeb, and it did not leave the NPT — the options it floated on Tuesday. It closed the one strait it physically commands, converting every southern-lane transit into a claimed violation of Iranian law.

“Note what Iran executed: not the options it floated — the one option it fully controls.”
